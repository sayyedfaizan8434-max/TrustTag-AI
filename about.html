🛡️ About TrustTag AI

A smart product verification system designed to help users identify suspicious products.

🔍 What is TrustTag AI?

TrustTag AI is a product verification platform that helps users check the authenticity of products using a Product ID, QR code or barcode.

⚙️ How It Works

1️⃣

Scan or Enter Product ID

Scan the QR/barcode or manually enter the unique Product ID.

2️⃣

Product Verification

TrustTag AI checks the product information and evaluates its verification status.

3️⃣

View Result

The system displays whether the product is genuine or suspicious.

4️⃣

Report Suspicious Product

Users can submit a report if they believe a product may be counterfeit.

⭐ Key Features

  • 🔍 Product ID Verification
  • 📷 QR / Barcode Scanning
  • ✅ Genuine Product Detection
  • ⚠️ Suspicious Product Detection
  • 🚨 Product Reporting
  • 📋 Report Management

🎯 Our Goal

The goal of TrustTag AI is to make product verification simple, fast and accessible while helping users identify potentially counterfeit products.
